## Replay result

Point-in-time replay of the revised technical selector at every original 18–20 Aug entry rejected all 10 historical losing entries because they lacked the newly required retest/momentum/support-resistance evidence. It also rejected the single historical winner, which is the expected conservative result from a `NO TRADE`-first safety change and is not evidence of positive expectancy. Exit-event replay found earlier protective events for the two overnight positions (`PFOCUS` at 09:46 IST and `EMMVEE` at 10:23 IST on 20 Aug) instead of next-day exits.

## Options audit

The retained Options state contains zero open or closed positions for the period, so there are no Options losses to attribute. The latest retained direction was `UNCLEAR` and no trade was opened. The revised Options entry path nevertheless adds explicit underlying trend, delta/strike, OI, volume, bid/ask spread, IV range/skew, theta-decay, DTE, premium-momentum, defined-risk, and risk/reward gates. After a loss it raises direction and momentum thresholds, narrows the allowed spread, and halves risk.
