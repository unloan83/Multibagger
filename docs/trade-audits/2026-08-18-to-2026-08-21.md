## Options

The retained Options store has no open or closed positions for these sessions, so there is no Options loss to attribute. Liquidity/spread, IV/theta, premium momentum and underlying-direction checks remain in code, but the complete Options execution path is now bypassed and its service/timer remain disabled.

## Readiness decision

Implemented and unit-tested does not mean ready. Historical loss rejection is material, but the retained replay lacks the newly required India VIX series and yields no accepted v4 trades. Paper/sandbox execution therefore remains paused. Resumption requires a current deployed market session with NIFTY, VIX and breadth, explicit `NO TRADE`/signal evidence, a complete sandbox entry-to-exit lifecycle, persistence/scheduler/kill-switch verification, and enough accepted shadow trades to measure net expectancy after costs.
